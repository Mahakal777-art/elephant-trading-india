# 14-day domestic B2B launch plan

Day 1: Verify Elephant Trading entity details, final GST advice, FSSAI scope and legal contact.
Day 2: Qualify 10 suppliers across 2 categories; collect GSTIN, relevant licences, samples, COA, MOQ, capacity and quotes.
Day 3: Qualify 20 buyers using public business directories and opt-in enquiries; record exact specifications.
Day 4: Supplier outreach in food ingredients and dehydrated products; request written commission terms.
Day 5: Buyer outreach to food processors, bakeries, snack makers and ingredient distributors; ask for real demand.
Day 6: Score only verified suppliers; reject document gaps and product mismatch.
Day 7: Obtain buyer spec sheets, target price, quantity, destination and delivery window in writing.
Day 8: Match suppliers; manually confirm capacity, MOQ, price and logistics.
Day 9: Send blind/teaser product options only; do not reveal buyer identity before signed referral terms.
Day 10: Execute supplier referral terms and buyer acknowledgement where needed.
Day 11: Introduce parties with consent; buyer and supplier negotiate directly.
Day 12: Track sample acceptance, quotation and purchase-order status.
Day 13: Confirm buyer payment/supplier dispatch status with evidence; issue commission invoice only after CA-reviewed tax treatment.
Day 14: Reconcile expected commission vs received bank credit; follow up on overdue payment; document repeat-order protection.

Daily metrics: 10 qualified new supplier prospects, 15 relevant buyer prospects, 10 personalized outreach messages, 5 follow-ups, 100% duplicate check, 100% source URLs saved. These are workload targets, not guaranteed conversions.
