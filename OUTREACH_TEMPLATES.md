# Elephant Trading — Manual B2B outreach templates

Personalize every message. Do not claim stock/certificates without supplier evidence. Buyer and supplier transact directly; intermediary commission only under signed written terms.

## English — Supplier
Subject: Domestic B2B sourcing enquiries for [product]

Hello [Name],

Elephant Trading is building an India-focused supplier network for [product/category]. Please share your specifications, MOQ, monthly capacity, dispatch location, GSTIN and relevant product licences/COA. Documents can be shared manually after initial contact; this website does not accept document uploads.

We act only as an intermediary. Buyer and supplier contract and invoice directly. Any introduction commission is agreed in writing before introduction. No deposit is requested.

Regards,
Elephant Trading
