How It Works

  1. Buyer submits a requirement.
  2. Supplier submits product, capacity and compliance details.
  3. Elephant Trading manually reviews supplier documents.
  4. Admin suggests and approves a buyer-supplier match.
  5. Parties agree price and transact directly.
  6. Commission is paid to Elephant Trading only under a written agreement.

Elephant Trading does not take title to, store, manufacture or resell goods.
